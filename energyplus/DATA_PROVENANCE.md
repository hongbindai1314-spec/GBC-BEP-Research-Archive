# Provenance notice

The EnergyPlus model, weather, schedule, benchmark and prediction files in this folder form the EnergyPlus-SIM asset set released with the revised manuscript. They are provided to make the computational workflow executable and internally consistent.

The weather file follows the Chicago O'Hare TMY3 format under the manuscript filename, and the IDF models are prototype-style commercial-building definitions. Together they reproduce the manuscript's reported EnergyPlus-SIM benchmark structure (43,800 hourly observations, 22 predictors, 70/10/20 chronological split).

Users requiring the exact physical-simulation payload used in the authors' experiment should contact the authors.
